✨ Simple Process

How ShareHands Works

Getting started is easy. Whether you're a donor, recipient, or NGO — our platform makes giving and receiving seamless.

📝

Create an Account

Sign up as a Donor, NGO, or Admin in under 2 minutes. Verify your identity and get started.

📦

List or Request Items

Donors list items they no longer need. NGOs and recipients browse and request what their community needs most.

✅

Get Approved

Our admin team reviews listings and requests to ensure quality and legitimacy before connecting parties.

🤝

Deliver & Impact

Items are picked up or delivered to centers. Donors get real-time updates and impact reports.

👥 Our Community

Who We Serve

Share Hands is built for everyone who wants to give or receive with dignity and ease.

🤝

Donors

Have clothes, books, furniture, or electronics you no longer use? List them in minutes and see them go to someone who truly needs them. Track your impact in real time.

🏢

NGOs & Charities

Partner with Share Hands to source donated goods for the communities you serve. Manage requests, track fulfillment, and measure your outreach impact — all in one place.

🔑

Administrators

Platform admins verify users, approve donations, manage NGO partnerships, oversee logistics, and ensure the highest standards of transparency and trust across the platform.
